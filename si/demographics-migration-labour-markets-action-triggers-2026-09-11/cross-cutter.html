Action Triggers · Demographics, Migration & Labour Markets · 11 September 2026

Nobody reopened the border. Every government counted who was already inside.

Over this cycle, the labour constraint changed shape. Nine weeks ago the central question was whether the workers existed at all: the domestic reservoir was draining, the import valve had jammed shut across the US, UK and EU at once, and the sending countries were ageing on the same clock. They exist. Spain had 337,917 regularised workers on its social security rolls by the end of August, Europe holds 4.41 million people under temporary protection it is now converting instead of returning, and euro-area workers aged 55 to 74 delivered 1.4 of the 1.7 percentage point rise in the employment rate. What is scarce is not people. It is permission, and the rules that grant it now carry numbers, prices and expiry dates. Below: five early warnings, six assumptions graded, and four actions now decision-shaped.

Early warnings

Signals the portfolio surfaced before consensus formed, in the order they hardened.

Lead time 3 weeks aheadSignal Scan Decision Intelligence

The payroll number everyone reads as a demand signal is now mostly denominator

Initial signal

We flagged in mid-August that the benchmark against which employment data is judged had become the most volatile input in the measurement, and that a planning rule keyed to a fixed payroll threshold was reading the wrong quantity.

Now evident

Three weeks later the instability could be sized, and it is larger than the signal it sits inside.

  • Break-even: US break-even employment has fallen from about 250,000 jobs a month in 2023 to roughly minus 3,000 in late 2025, and is forecast near zero through 2027 and slightly negative by 2028.
  • Revision noise: population-vintage revisions alone move break-even estimates by up to 66,000 jobs a month, around 60% of mean monthly gains, and contemporaneous 2026 estimates differ by roughly 50,000.
  • Participation: a single January 2026 population-control revision accounted for 43% of this year's fall in US participation.
  • UK: the statistical authority has stopped seeking accredited status for its flagship labour survey outputs.
Decide

Any 2027 forecast that reads a weak payroll print as weak demand is comparing this year's numerator with an older year's denominator.

Lead time 4 weeks aheadSignal Scan Decision Intelligence

Britain set the migrant wage floor by arithmetic before it published the allocation rule

Initial signal

We identified in early August that Britain had begun setting the pay floor for sponsored mid-skill work by a lifetime fiscal calculation which assumes the worker never settles, so the wage was being indexed to Treasury arithmetic and not to the labour market it was supposed to clear.

Now evident

By September the allocation rule itself was published and numerical, and it binds on employers whether or not the fiscal argument behind it survives contest.

  • Threshold: the general Skilled Worker salary threshold is now derived from the 30th percentile of ASHE 2025 earnings, at GBP 44,000.
  • Shortage list: Temporary Shortage List access was recommended for 28 of 82 occupations, for eighteen months only, none of them for full access.
  • Volume: roughly 10,000 visas a year cut to about 4,000, conditional on an employer plan to maximise use of the UK workforce.
  • Settlement: settlement is being pushed out to a decade or more, so the sponsored workforce becomes employer-tied and largely immobile by design.

Decide

Sponsorship is now a priced and time-limited licence, so the workforce plan needs a renewal calendar and not a headcount target.

Lead time 7 weeks aheadSignal Scan Decision Intelligence

Labour visas entered foreign policy while the coverage was still about borders

Initial signal

In mid-July we read the EU's new legal labour channels as sitting inside the same policy circuit as returns, at a point when the Pact was being covered as a borders and asylum story. We were explicit that this was a capability and not yet a codified rule.

Now evident

The capability was named at the top of the Commission's own instrument, and the September continuity ledger had to re-point the watch item the July cycle had set.

  • Return Regulation: the 1 June political agreement names visa and trade policy as incentives in migration diplomacy.
  • Watch item moved: the July cycle asked us to watch sector-by-sector migration windows; the instrument that actually moved was returns.
  • Talent Pool: the competing channel remains voluntary, with no conditionality codified and the platform not fully operational until the end of 2027.
Prepare

Any sourcing corridor that runs through a single origin country now carries a foreign-policy exposure the workforce plan does not currently price.

Lead time 8 weeks aheadSignal Scan Decision Intelligence

Older workers were carrying euro-area employment growth while our own briefing called the lever spent

Initial signal

Three days after the July briefing judged the domestic participation lever largely pulled, our own scan argued the opposite: that later retirement was doing more than migration to fill Europe's labour gap. We published the contradiction rather than reconciling it.

Now evident

The scan was right and the briefing was wrong, which the September continuity ledger grades BROKEN against its own prior finding.

  • Euro area: workers aged 55 to 74 delivered 1.4 of the 1.7 percentage point rise in the employment rate, with more than 90% of the older-worker gain attributed to higher participation.
  • Spain: 482,000 of 679,000 new affiliates in a year were foreign nationals already resident, so the recorded gain came from status and not from arrival.
  • Germany: exempting the first EUR 2,000 a month of earnings past pension age moved roughly 9,000 people, against a demographic loss of 470,000 in the same year.
Decide

The participation reserve is real and it does not respond to pay, so any plan that priced it as exhausted, or that tries to buy it back, is mis-specified in both directions.

Lead time Pre-consensusSignal Scan

Germany rebuilt the machinery for claiming young workers before deciding to use it

Initial signal

In early August we read Germany's rearmament as quietly rebuilding a labour-allocation institution and not only as equipment procurement, and named the trigger as a recruitment shortfall.

Now evident

The machinery is financed and dated while the decision to use it is not, which is the exposure: the trigger sits with recruitment performance and no security event is required to pull it.

  • Enumeration: a birth cohort has been counted and registered.
  • Screening: nationwide compulsory medical screening is financed for 2027.
  • Powers held: a demand-triggered draft and a revocable exit-permission power are held in reserve.

Assumptions that have shifted

Cycle-over-cycle assumption changes, in decreasing order of strategic weight.

Shift 1 of 6 Replaced

There were no workers left to plan for

Prior cycle held

With the domestic reservoir draining and the import valve jammed by political design, the July edition closed on existence: "the load-bearing variable for every H2 2026 workforce plan has changed: not the cost of labour, but whether the workers exist to be planned for at all". That was the question put to every plan.

This cycle reads

They are there, and this cycle counted them. What is rationed is legal access, which is a different variable with different owners and different dates.

  • Spain: 337,917 regularised workers affiliated to social security at 31 August 2026, from nearly 1.2 million applications against an expectation of around 500,000.
  • Composition: foreign nationals supplied 71% of the year's affiliation gain and all of August's seasonally adjusted rise, while affiliation among Spanish nationals fell.
  • Europe: 4.41 million people under temporary protection are being converted into settled labour supply through work-based transition statuses, with the scheme extended to March 2028.
  • Design limit: the Spanish permits run one year and convert to ordinary residence only through employment or registered job search.
Forward thesis

Plan against permission and its expiry, not against the size of the pool. The tripwire is the spring 2027 Spanish renewal window, where one-year permits either convert or lapse.

Shift 2 of 6 Overturned

Older workers had nothing left to give

Prior cycle held

The July briefing put it plainly: "the last cycle's participation lever (women, older workers, disability) has largely been pulled". That is what left migration carrying the whole adjustment and made the political ceiling look binding.

This cycle reads

The reserve was not spent, and the cycle also produced a clean test of what moves it. Germany exempted the first EUR 2,000 a month of earnings past statutory pension age from tax from 1 January 2026 and moved roughly 9,000 people against a demographic loss of 470,000, so price is not what binds. Employer practice is.

Forward thesis

Treat older-worker participation as a conditions problem owned by operations, not a fiscal problem owned by treasuries. The tripwire is the first government to move an employer-practice instrument, on hours, role design or phased retirement, ahead of the 2027 budget round.

Shift 3 of 6 Overturned

Fewer migrants would mean the same jobs filled by other people

Prior cycle held

The July briefing framed the turn as "immigration policy has moved from lever to constraint", and read restriction as a squeeze on supply: fewer inflows, the same jobs, a harder contest for who fills them. That reading carries an unexamined premise, which is that the work survives the worker.

This cycle reads

Restriction is subtracting from employment rather than reallocating it, and the losses land on native-born and immigrant workers alike.

  • First causal estimate: 1.3% fewer US-born men with a high school degree or less in work after an enforcement surge, and 3% fewer in construction.
  • Care first: health care added 22,000 jobs in July against a 36,000 monthly average, with protected status lapsed for roughly 330,000 Haitians.
  • Stock: the US foreign-born workforce fell 122,000 over the year to January 2026, with net international migration down in every state and in 90% of 3,144 counties.
Forward thesis

Model removal as capacity loss in the receiving sector, not as a vacancy transferred to a domestic worker. The tripwire is the first quarter in which a restricting economy's care or construction employment falls while its unemployment rate also falls.

Shift 4 of 6 Replaced

Governments would haggle over exemptions, industry by industry

Prior cycle held

The July edition's instruction was to "plan against the ceiling, not the pact: the negotiating currency of the next two years is the sector carve-out, not the bilateral agreement". The reading behind it was that governments would hold the overall limit and trade exceptions beneath it.

This cycle reads

The carve-out arrived and it is not negotiated. It is published, conditional and time-boxed, and in Japan it is a number that no longer adjusts to demand.

  • United Kingdom: shortage access granted for eighteen months, to 28 of 82 occupations, none at full access, and conditional on an employer plan to increase use of the domestic workforce.
  • Japan: a food-service acceptance projection of 50,000 applied as a ceiling, roughly 46,000 already resident at end-February, and eligibility certificates refused from 13 April 2026.
  • Context: the same year Japan's foreign resident population passed four million for the first time.
Forward thesis

The currency is the published rule and the date it expires, so the negotiation that matters is about renewal terms and not about entry. The tripwire is the first eighteen-month shortage-list expiry, and whether Japan raises the 50,000 figure administratively.

Shift 5 of 6 Split

Japan was the one country quietly opening its doors

Prior cycle held

Japan was named as the divergence, expanding quietly under a restrictive headline while the Atlantic economies closed in unison. The instruction was to "track Japan as the leading indicator for ageing-economy labour policy, with named triggers for whether Korea or Australia follow the quiet-expansion playbook".

This cycle reads

Half of that held. Japan is still the leading indicator, and it moved first again. The direction did not hold: the quiet expansion became an enforced cap in the same year the resident population passed four million, and a government expert panel on future acceptance opened on 7 August with a basic policy due inside the fiscal year.

Forward thesis

Keep Japan as the indicator and re-point the trigger from expansion to whether an administrative ceiling can be lifted once a sector has planned around it. The tripwire is the expert panel's basic policy, due before 31 March 2027.

Shift 6 of 6 Weakened

Automation would cover the desk jobs and shortages would hit the rest

Prior cycle held

The July edition asked planners to "split the workforce plan in two: capital-intensive functions get faster and cheaper, human-intensive functions get scarcer and more expensive on wage and subsidy lines alike". Automation was expected to land in cognitive work while the demographic pressure sat in care, construction and trades.

This cycle reads

Still directionally sound and no longer the split that bites. No verifiable new measurement of the offset entered the evidence base this cycle, so the momentum register retired it. What did bite was geography and legal status: shortage and slack sitting side by side inside one country where national levers cannot reach them, and a sponsored workforce made employer-tied by settlement design.

Forward thesis

Keep the sectoral split as a cost assumption and add a mobility axis to the siting decision. The tripwire is the first national measurement of the automation offset to enter the base since May 2026.

Actions triggered

Each trigger names why it activates now, why it matters, the recommended actions, the primary decision owner, the decision window, and the conditions under which the recommendation should be reviewed.

Decide Action 1 Signal Scan Decision Intelligence

Re-base the workforce plan on who you can keep and convert, and put a price on the conversion

Trigger condition

This recommendation has been activated because the largest single addition to a mature European labour supply this year came from people already resident rather than from recruitment, with 337,917 regularised workers on the Spanish social security rolls at 31 August 2026 and foreign nationals supplying 71% of the year's affiliation gain, because Europe is converting 4.41 million people under temporary protection into settled labour supply through work-based transition statuses instead of returning them, and because the statuses doing that work carry expiry dates that sit in an immigration file rather than in the workforce plan.

Primary decision ownerCHRO Decision windowImmediateFY27 plan, by 31 December 2026
Why this now matters

A plan built on hiring volume assumes replacements are purchasable at a price. In this cycle's evidence they are not, and the people who would have been hired are already on site holding a permission somebody else can decline to renew.

Recommended actions
  • Immediate: count how many of your people hold a status with an expiry date, and put every one of those dates on the same calendar as the operating plan.
  • Immediate: identify the roles where a lapsed renewal takes out a shift rather than a headcount, and name a single accountable owner for each.
  • Next quarter: price conversion support, on legal cost, wage uplift to threshold and time to competence, against the cost of replacement recruitment at current visa rules.
  • Follow-on: move the retention target from annual turnover to survival through the next renewal point, and report it that way.
Review conditions

This recommendation should be reviewed if a receiving economy converts a temporary status to indefinite leave at scale, which would remove the renewal cliff this rests on, if the spring 2027 Spanish renewal window shows conversion rates above 80%, which would narrow it to the sectors that fail that test, or if a coordinated reopening of recruitment routes restores hiring as the cheaper path.

Write the domestic-workforce plan before you need the visa

Trigger condition

This recommendation has been activated because shortage-list access in the UK is now conditional on an employer plan to maximise use of the domestic workforce and is granted for eighteen months only, to 28 of 82 occupations and none at full access, because the volume behind that door fell from roughly 10,000 visas a year to about 4,000, and because the plan itself is the part no employer has been asked for before and therefore the part nobody has drafted.

Primary decision ownerCHRO with Public Affairs Decision windowNext quarterDrafted before the next list review
Why this now matters

Access has been converted into a document you either have or do not have on the day the window opens. Employers who start drafting when the shortage bites will be writing a training commitment under time pressure, against competitors who filed one already.

Recommended actions
  • Immediate: list the occupations you sponsor and check each against the 30th percentile of ASHE 2025 earnings, at GBP 44,000 for the general threshold.
  • Next quarter: draft the domestic-workforce plan as a real training and progression commitment with named intakes, because it will be read as one.
  • Next quarter: put every rule-reset date into the planning calendar: the eighteen-month list expiry, the spring 2027 Spanish renewal window, the March 2028 temporary protection end and Japan's basic policy before 31 March 2027.
  • Follow-on: map which sourcing corridors run through a single origin country, now that visa and trade policy are named incentives in returns diplomacy.
Review conditions

This recommendation should be reviewed if the conditional element is dropped from shortage-list access, which would return the decision to price alone, if the list is extended beyond eighteen months, which would push it into the next planning cycle, or if your sponsored roles clear the percentile threshold on their own wage growth and the access question stops binding.

State the break-even employment assumption your forecasts use, and put a range on it

Trigger condition

This recommendation has been activated because US break-even employment has fallen from about 250,000 jobs a month in 2023 to roughly minus 3,000 in late 2025 and is forecast near zero through 2027, because population-vintage revisions alone move the estimate by up to 66,000 jobs a month, around 60% of mean monthly gains, and because almost no forecast states the threshold it is using, so a number that has moved by more than its own signal is being carried as an unwritten assumption.

Primary decision ownerCFO with Head of Strategy Decision windowNext planning cycleFY27 planning pack
Why this now matters

A demand read that is wrong by 66,000 jobs a month is wrong by more than the average monthly print it is interpreting. Hiring plans, capacity decisions and rate expectations all inherit that error silently, because nobody wrote the assumption down to argue with.

Recommended actions
  • Immediate: write the break-even figure your demand commentary assumes into the planning pack as an explicit range, not a point estimate.
  • Next quarter: re-run last year's labour-market calls against the revised denominator and see which of them change sign.
  • Follow-on: add a standing note on which labour series have lost accreditation or changed vintage, so the range can be widened before a decision rests on it.
Review conditions

This recommendation should be reviewed if a statistical agency publishes a reconciled population vintage that narrows the estimate spread, which would let a point estimate return, if break-even turns durably negative, which would escalate this to a Decide because employment growth would no longer be the right measure at all, or if your own exposure is concentrated in economies where the denominator has not moved.

Put Germany's claim on young technical labour into the site and succession plan

Trigger condition

The exposure is not the draft. It is that the machinery exists, is dated and is triggered by a number the state controls, so the decision can arrive between two planning rounds in the age band that carries apprenticeship intakes and early technical progression.

Recommended actions
  • Immediate: size the share of your German apprentice and early-career technical population inside the enumerated cohort.

The body of work, in one sentence

Over this cycle, the binding constraint moved from supply to permission. The question in July was whether advanced economies could still buy the workers they needed. They have stopped trying, and have started allocating instead: a published percentile, an enforced sectoral ceiling, a one-year permit, a conditional shortage list, a tax exemption. The people are there, and this cycle counted them: 4.41 million under temporary protection in Europe, 337,917 newly on the Spanish rolls, a 55 to 74 cohort still carrying euro-area employment growth after we had written that lever off. The load-bearing variable for every 2027 plan is no longer the size of the pool. It is who holds the rule that governs access to it, and the date that rule is next re-set.
