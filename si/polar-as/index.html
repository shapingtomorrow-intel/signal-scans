Polar A/S

Money cleared, capacity is the question.

An evolving record of the strategic-foresight work we are doing for Polar A/S (Skanderborg, Denmark) on the four pressures arriving together in H2 2026: automotive-fluids volume shift, channel and e-commerce concentration on commodity SKUs, the EU regulatory calendar through 2026-2027, and the fixed-cost economics of two Esbjerg sites plus one Skanderborg site. Three product formats: a monthly Change Tracker on momentum across the topics, a Decision Intelligence briefing per quarter, and a cycle-end Action Triggers brief that synthesises the work into named decisions for the planning round. All deliverables in English and Danish.
