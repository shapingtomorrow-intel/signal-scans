Early warnings

Four developments the cycle has surfaced ahead of board consensus, in the order they harden.

Hard deadline inside the cycle Tracker row 6

REACH Annex XVII closure on DMAC and NEP binds 23 December 2026

The early read · 3 June 2025

Commission Regulation (EU) 2025/1090 added DMAC and NEP to REACH Annex XVII at 0.3% w/w with DNEL-based occupational controls. Joins NMP (2018) and DMF (2021) in the dipolar-aprotic-amide closed group.

Main compliance23 Dec 2026 NEP fibre derogation23 June 2029 Direct hit on Polar SKUsLow (catalogue does not list these)
Why it still matters

The regulatory direction-of-travel is established. Polar's solvent catalogue (acetone, IPA, white spirit, xylene, bioethanol) is one cycle behind in the queue. The next REACH ATP cycle is the trigger to plan for.

Prepare

Audit the catalogue against the SVHC Candidate List (n-hexane added 4 February 2026) and the Restrictions Roadmap (CMRs, EDs, PMT/vPvM prioritised 2026-2027).

Pre-consensus · Greek CLH dossier due end-2026 Tracker cluster 2

Ethanol CMR Cat 1A / Repr 1A re-classification is the live risk to the compliance-moat thesis

The early read · February 2026

ECHA's Biocides Committee gave a positive ethanol opinion in PT1, PT2, PT4 in February 2026 (a defensible moat for Article 95 suppliers). Greece's CLH dossier proposing CMR Cat 1A / Repr 1A is due end-2026.

Why it is pre-consensus

If RAC adopts the Greek classification in 2027, the downstream labelling and authorisation reset hits every ethanol-based household, disinfectant and PT1/PT2/PT4 biocide product in the EU. The compliance-moat reading of cycle 1 resets.

Monitor

Trigger to escalate to Prepare: Greek CLH dossier submitted to ECHA before end-2026.

Pre-consensus in Denmark Tracker row 2

Denmark's 81.6% February 2026 EV share is 5-10 years ahead of the EU and reshapes the aftermarket cycle now

The early read · January-February 2026

Denmark 68.5% BEV share of new car sales 2025; 81.6% in February 2026 (EAFO). EU 17.4% (2025) and 20% YTD May 2026 (ACEA). Kline projects only ~15% BEV in EU parc by 2034.

What this means for Polar specifically

The Danish workshop channel re-stocks one cycle ahead of EU peers. Polar's domestic distributor relationships (SB Værktøj named; FTZ, Thansen, Autoplus unverified) are where the volume mix re-bases first.

Prepare

Define the EV-coolant qualification track now; the window with Nordic OEM workshops is open through 2027.

Already hardening Tracker rows 4 + 5

Three Nordic distributors at €20.7bn, DKK 12bn and SEK 51bn are absorbing the channel

The early read · Q1-Q2 2026

Würth EUR 20.7bn 2025 sales, e-business at 25.2%, Chemicals business unit +6.7%. Solar A/S (Denmark) DKK ~12bn pushing e-commerce + AI logistics. Ahlsell under CVC, 100+ acquisitions. Plus Würth Denmark, FTZ, Stark, Sanistål, IKH-Damixa locally.

Why it is a near-term margin event, not a 2030 trend

B2B e-commerce is only 4.34% of EU enterprise turnover (Eurostat 2024) but rising; price transparency on commodity SKUs (screenwash, MEG/MPG coolants, AdBlue, household cleaners) compounds inside the cycle. Branded commodity margin is moving channel-ward now.

Decide

Which SKUs concede price to channel and which differentiate (workshop-spec lock-in, BPR Article 95 lock-in) within the next planning round.

Working assumptions worth re-testing this cycle

Three assumptions a small Danish chemicals blender might be carrying forward that the cycle's evidence challenges.

Shift 1 of 3

"The 55-SKU range each earns its keep across three sites"

The implicit prior

Three sites + 55 SKUs + 25-year operating history equals a coherent footprint that pays its way.

June 2026 reads

Cefic puts EU27 chemicals utilisation at ~74% in 2025; the threshold to cover fixed costs sits at ~80-85%. Danish electricity is EUR 34.9/100 kWh vs EU average EUR 28.7; Industriens Overenskomst 2025-2028 locks in wage steps of 4.0%/3.5%/3.5%. The benchmark Polar is priced against has moved.

Forward thesis

The question for the FY27 plan is not whether to consolidate but which subset of the 55 SKUs earns the three-site burden and which migrate to tolling, channel partner or sunset.

Shift 2 of 3

"Compliance is a cost; branded SKUs win on quality"

The implicit prior

Regulatory cost is a tax to be minimised; the branded Polar mark earns trust premium with workshops and the agricultural trade.

June 2026 reads

The BPR Union authorisation fee rose to EUR 179,250 in August 2025; Article 95 supplier verification is now a 6-12 month process; ECHA's Restrictions Roadmap prioritises CMRs, EDs, PBT/vPvB and PMT/vPvM through 2026-2027. Compliance lock-in is the moat private label and Amazon Business cannot easily cross.

Forward thesis

Treat compliance not as cost but as the strategic moat the catalogue restructure migrates into. The SKU prune is not just "which earns its keep" but "which fits inside the regulated lock-in".

Shift 3 of 3

"Esbjerg is a chemicals-friendly industrial town"

The implicit prior

Esbjerg's oil and gas legacy makes it a natural home for chemicals tenants on Mådevej and Brolæggervej.

June 2026 reads

Port Esbjerg has pivoted decisively to offshore wind, Power-to-X and CO2 logistics. H2 Energy Europe (1 GW hydrogen) and Hoest/CIP (ammonia from 2027) anchor DKK 500m+ of new infrastructure. The WEF named Port Esbjerg a Transitioning Industrial Cluster. Chemical tenants now compete for land and grid with much higher-value green-fuels projects.

Forward thesis

Esbjerg's industrial vitality is a tailwind for the cluster but a headwind for the Polar cost line. Expect rising land cost, longer grid queue and harder labour competition from PtX wages.

Decisions decision-shaped this cycle

Four moves the FY27 planning round can act on with posture and named owner.

Decide Action 1 Tracker pattern 1

Run a SKU profitability + compliance-fit screen on the full 55 SKUs by end Q3 2026

OwnerOwner-CEO + CFO DeadlineEnd Q3 2026 OutputThree lists: keep in-house / toll / sunset
Why decide now

The cost-curve, channel and regulatory pressures are now all moving the same direction. Acting on the FY27 plan with last cycle's SKU mix is the costliest option.

The screen, in three filters
  • Gross margin per SKU at full fully-loaded site cost (energy + labour 2027 + compliance).
  • Compliance-moat fit: BPR Article 95, ECHA Restrictions Roadmap exposure, CLP 23rd ATP relabel cost.
  • Channel concentration risk: single-distributor exposure, private-label price-floor reach.

Prepare Action 2 Tracker pattern 3

Stand up a tolling option for two SKU families by end FY26

OwnerOperations Director ReferenceUmicore Bruges + European parallels Decision gateSingle-line economics vs in-house
Why prepare now

The tolling option preserves the customer relationship while testing whether the SKU profitably leaves the three-site footprint. The European toller market is now the European specialty-chemical default for sub-scale firms.

Preparing means three things
  • Pick two SKU families with stable demand but commodity margin (likely a coolant grade and a household solvent).
  • Run RFQs against three named European tollers.
  • Build the customer-communication and quality-control architecture before any tolling pilot ships.
Trigger to escalate to Decide

Tolled-route fully loaded cost lands at <90% of in-house fully loaded cost on the pilot SKU.

Prepare Action 3 Tracker row 9

Build the EV-coolant qualification track with two Nordic OEM-workshop chains by end FY27

OwnerTechnical Director + Sales WindowThrough 2027 ReferenceBASF Glysantin Electrified (Sep 2025)
Why prepare now

EV thermal management requires dielectric, low-conductivity coolants on a specification base different from Polar's MEG/MPG line. The qualification cycle through Nordic OEM workshops and EV-aftermarket chains is open for a small number of regional blenders.

Preparing means three things
  • Source a dielectric base from a qualified European producer.
  • Run a six-month workshop pilot with one named OEM-aligned chain.
  • Build the technical-data sheet and warranty wording aligned to ACEA/OEM 2024 sequences.

Monitor Standing watch Tracker wild card 2

Strategic-buyer optionality: track Nordic specialty-chemicals M&A and define the "good acquirer" profile

Three named markers
  • Plum-Kiilto integration progress (Finnish strategic into Danish hygiene/cleaning).
  • Krüger-Veolia capacity moves in Danish water-treatment chemicals.
  • Borup Kemi or DanKemi sale signal (private founder companies, similar profile to Polar).
Monitor

Any of the three turning sharply is a trigger to commission a board-level optionality review on whether to plan as a continuing independent or as a clean acquisition target on a 24-month horizon.

The body of work, in one sentence

The cycle says Polar's four pressures are simultaneous and all pointing the same way; the strategic question for the FY27 plan is not which pressure to address first but which subset of the 55-SKU range earns the three-site fixed-cost burden, and which routes (compliance-moat retention, tolling, channel re-set, or clean disposal) buy the most time.
