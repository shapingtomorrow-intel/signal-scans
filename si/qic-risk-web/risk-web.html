Two shifts from the portfolio web reach Tilt directly: higher real interest rates, through the cost of debt, and who stands behind renewable revenue, through AGL. A third, grid limits on new wind and solar, appears on Tilt’s web but not yet on the portfolio web. That is exactly the gap the unpriced-shifts register is for.

3. What a running programme would add

  • Every colour re-tested each month against new evidence, with a small triangle showing which way it moved since the last edition.
  • Thresholds agreed with the deal team. When an assumption turns red, or crosses a threshold, it goes on the agenda as an Action Trigger, with an owner and a date.
  • Shifts that no assumption covers, such as the grid limits above, go on the unpriced-shifts register for the investment committee.

How this example was built

  • Built on 27 public sources, each with a dated page: 15 from regulators, central banks, market operators and company disclosures, 2 from research, and 10 from quality journalism and trade press.
  • Traced all 15 figures in the text to the exact wording of a source.
  • Every quote is checked against the live page before this page is published.
  • Holdings are never coloured. Colour sits on the lines and on Tilt’s assumptions, so the page shows what the evidence says, not a judgement of any business.
