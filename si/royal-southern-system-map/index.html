The Decision Intelligence briefing says what is changing for the Royal Southern Yacht Club to 2040. This System Map shows why the pieces move together: which changes feed each other, which push back, and where the club’s own decisions change how the whole system behaves rather than one part of it.

Read it by following the arrows. A + means the two move in the same direction; a − means they move in opposite directions. Where the arrows close into a circle, they form a loop: an R loop reinforces itself, so change feeds on change, and a B loop balances, pushing back against change. Turning

R1 Dearer boats, fewer owners

Brokers say a £20,000 boat that costs £5,000 a year to park makes buyers stop (Marine Industry News, May 2026), and marina operators report affordability challenges among an ageing customer base (British Marine, Jan 2026). Every step has current evidence, so the loop is turning.

Watch: berth occupancy and waiting lists on the Hamble, and the age profile of new members.

Priming

R2 Supplied boats bring new sailors

Supplied-boat leagues run in Britain (British Keelboat League, Jul 2026) and Germany (YACHT, in German, Oct 2026), but no Solent club yet runs a supplied fleet at scale, so the circle has not closed here.

Watch: the number of club and university teams in supplied-boat leagues, and young members who keep racing after junior classes.

Priming

B1 Protection closes anchorages

Chichester Harbour banned anchoring over a native oyster site from April 2026 (Chichester Harbour Conservancy, Apr 2026), and seagrass has been planted on the Hamble (Hampshire and Isle of Wight Wildlife Trust, Jul 2026). The restriction is in place in one harbour; the loop has not yet reached the Hamble.

Watch: new prohibited anchorages in the Solent, and any rule attached to the Hamble's seagrass site.

Priming

R3 Chargers bring electric boats

Lymington Harbour has installed a 75kW fast charger (Marine Industry News, Oct 2026), but British Marine says the rules on reselling electricity give marinas no incentive to invest (British Marine, Nov 2025).

Watch: Ofgem's decision on resold electricity, and charger use at the Electric Seaway sites.

Priming

B2 Fire risk slows batteries

Marine insurers say yacht fires spread to neighbouring boats and marina infrastructure, and that breaching battery standards may lead to withdrawal of cover (International Union of Marine Insurance, Jun 2026).

Watch: insurers' or The Yacht Harbour Association's rules for batteries on berths.

What the map shows that the briefing alone cannot
  1. Cost can feed the club or drain it. Rising cost drives loop R1 (dearer boats, fewer owners), which drains the club. The same cost drives people towards supplied boats, which feeds loop R2. Which loop wins depends on whether the club supplies the boats, and that is the horizontal axis of the Scenario Matrix.
  2. The Solent's limits act on owners first. Loop B1 (protection closes anchorages) and rising seas act on the boats members own and the places they anchor and moor. A club whose members sail supplied boats from its own marina is less exposed to both.
  3. The marina's constraint is power, not boats. Loops R3 and B2 both run through the marina’s power and safety rules. Electric boats will arrive at their own pace; the club’s choice is whether its grid capacity and battery rules are ready when they do.

7. How the club would use it

The value of a System Map comes as much from arguing over the arrows as from the finished picture. This draft is a starting point:

Challenge sessionOne facilitated session of about 90 minutes in which flag officers, committee chairs and the marina team test every arrow, add what the evidence missed and agree the leverage points. Their corrections become part of the map.
Committee lensesThe same structure, shaded for each committee: the loops are shared, but sailing, membership, house and marina see different arrows as their own.
Other questionsThe clubhouse, events or the junior programme can be mapped the same way, so the Executive Committee sees one method across its decisions.
