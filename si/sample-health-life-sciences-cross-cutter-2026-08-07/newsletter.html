Medicine became industrial policy
On 2 April a 100 percent US pharma tariff arrived as a tiered 100/20/0 menu tied to price cuts and onshoring. Seventeen manufacturers covering 86 percent of the branded market signed pricing deals, and the EU passed a Buy-European Critical Medicines Act.
