One rulebook, twenty-seven enforcers
Germany created a funded national AI supervisor on 29 July, four days before Article 50 and general-purpose AI enforcement went live. The Commission’s own guidance puts enforcement mainly with national authorities. As of March, eight of twenty-seven member states had notified one.

The deadline stopped being the plan
California approved its emissions reporting rule in February, withdrew it in June, re-proposed it in July, and moved the first deadline from 10 August to 10 November. The statutory assurance ramp, limited from 2026 and reasonable by 2030, did not move at all.

Liability opened a second front
The revised Product Liability Directive must be transposed by 9 December. It brings software inside the definition of a product, lets a claimant compel disclosure on a plausibility showing, and lets a court presume the defect where that disclosure is not met.
