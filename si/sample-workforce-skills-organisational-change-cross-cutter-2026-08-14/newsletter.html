Name the AI premium, or stop paying it
Two thirds of employers pay a premium for AI-skilled roles, and more than half of the people already doing that work saw pay flat or fall. Writing the criterion into the job architecture costs a revision now. Meeting it for the first time inside a pay-differential request costs the defence.

Fund internal mobility in the FY27 plan
Adult participation in education and training across the EU-27 sits at 39.5 percent, and roughly one in four mid-career professionals stops progressing around the ten-year mark. If nobody leaves, every progression promise becomes an internal delivery commitment, and delivery needs a lateral-move rate and a retraining number against it.
