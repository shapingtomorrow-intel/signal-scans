2027 Will Test Food and Water Buffers That Are Thinner Than They Look

The cushions for a bad year, from world grain stocks to Europe's farm reserve and stored water, are thinner or harder to reach than the totals suggest, and a very strong El Niño lands on them in 2027. January's insurance renewals and the spring harvest estimates will show how thin.

Early warnings

Lead time 3 weeks aheadSignal Scan Decision Intelligence

Buyers who size their cover to world grain stocks will find most of the cushion out of reach when El Niño peaks.

Initial signal

We identified that the headline stock totals include reserves that will not be traded, so the buffer open to importers is the exporters' line, which is smaller and falling.

Now evident
  • Who holds the corn: of 280.44 million tonnes of world corn stocks, China holds 165.13 million and is projected to export 0.02 million, while major exporters hold 25.11 million, down from 27.14 million a year earlier.
  • Prices: the FAO cereal index stood 17.2% above a year earlier in September, with maize at its highest in more than three years.
  • The peak: NOAA gives an 83% chance of a historic El Niño, stronger than any since 1950, between October and December, and the World Bank expects food commodity prices to rise another 7% in 2027.

India's winter harvest will be decided by its reservoirs, and an export curb would carry the shortfall abroad.

Initial signal

We read India's agriculture ministry writing its winter crop plan from reservoir water and soil moisture rather than from stock positions, while fertiliser stocks were ample.

Now evident
  • The plan: a rabi target of 177.72 million tonnes set with reservoir water and soil moisture named among its drivers, and four southern states told to plan crops on water availability.
  • The drought: 52.5% of India's area dry or in drought on 16 September, up from 38.9% a month earlier, after a monsoon at 87% of normal.
  • The reservoirs: the 178 reservoirs the government monitors held 16.44% less than the ten-year normal on 15 October.

Assumptions that have shifted

Cycle-over-cycle assumption changes, in decreasing order of strategic weight.

Shift 1 of 6 Replaced

Rules that take away access are the main climate risk to plan for

Prior cycle held

September's briefing was titled "Climate Policy Has Started Taking Things Away": water rights on short terms, plot-level origin evidence and carbon charges were read as the constraints that would bind first.

This cycle reads

The rules arrived on time but thin, unfunded or looser than written, while the physical squeeze moved first. El Niño, water and food prices now set the 2027 plan, and the rules decide who carries the cost.

  • Deforestation: enforcement in Germany is planned around a control rate of about 1.8% of importers.
  • Restoration: national plans become final in September 2027, before the Commission says what they will cost.
  • Carbon farming: the rulebook adopted on 9 July says it cannot yet quantify the land-price risk it may create, and defers the safeguards.
  • Physical: India's winter crop is being planned from water budgets, and Lake Kariba's water for power falls by about a third in 2027.
Forward thesis

Plan 2027 around physical buffers first and rules second. The tripwire is a grain-exporting government restricting wheat, maize or rice exports before 31 March 2027, or the FAO cereal index for November staying at or above September's 122.8 points.

Shift 2 of 6 Holding

Europe's drought will reach food prices through next year's contracts

Prior cycle held

September called it this way: "Europe's 2026 drought will push up food prices, so flat food inflation in August is not the reassurance it looks like", and the September synthesis graded food momentum as weakened at the till.

This cycle reads

The call held against a direct test. The FAO food price index averaged 136.0 points in September, 5.8% above a year earlier, wheat reached its highest since August 2023, and the World Bank expects food commodity prices to rise about 10% in 2026 and another 7% in 2027.

Forward thesis

Treat 2027 as a contract-cost year in food buying. The tripwire is the World Bank or FAO cutting its 2027 food price outlook below a rise, which would reopen the question.

Shift 3 of 6 Weakened

The length of a water right matters more than the size of the cut

Prior cycle held

The prior cycle held that "the binding change is the tenor of entitlement rather than the size of the cut", and split it into two mechanisms: administered terms and discretionary restriction.

This cycle reads

The short terms are real and contested: two-year cuts for 2027 and 2028, with Nevada and now Arizona in federal court. But for 2027 the volume is back as the binding fact: Lake Powell's releases fall to 6.00 to 7.00 million acre-feet and Kariba's water for power falls to 23 billion cubic metres from 36 billion. Where storage refilled, as in Spain at 59.2% full, a dry season can still be absorbed.

Forward thesis

Test each site on both the term and the volume of its water. The tripwire is Reclamation's next 24-month study keeping Lake Powell in its lowest operating range, expected by April 2027.

Shift 4 of 6 Split

The 30 December deforestation date holds, so the risk is the deadline

Prior cycle held

September's call was that "the EU's 30 December 2026 deadline for deforestation-free supply chains will hold", and the September synthesis told procurement leads to scope every product line against it.

This cycle reads

The date is holding: the Commission still stresses the timetable. But the risk has moved from the deadline to the member state where goods first enter the EU, and it is thin and severe at once.

  • Germany: the bill assumes inspecting about 1.8% of 33,000 to 126,000 importers, yet allows up to five years' imprisonment.
  • Ireland: the scheme allows fines of up to €10m or 4% of EU-wide turnover, and personal liability for officers.
  • Cost: the Commission puts annual compliance costs at EUR 2.0 billion, down from EUR 8.1 billion, with downstream operators largely relieved.
Forward thesis

Price a failure by the member state of first placement, not by the odds of an audit. The tripwire is a German authority announcing its first fine proceeding under the Act before 30 June 2027.

Shift 5 of 6 Weakened

Carbon is now a cost companies pay, not just a figure they report

Prior cycle held

September held that "carbon costs are now being charged, not just reported", on border certificate prices and national auctions in Germany, the United Kingdom and Canada.

This cycle reads

Carbon is still charged today, but the 2030s price is being marked down. The Commission's July proposal cuts the 2031 to 2035 reduction factor from 4.4% to 3.7% and halves the reserve's intake, Sandbag puts the 2040 surplus at 4.4 times annual emissions, and ABN AMRO now forecasts 91 EUR/tCO2 for 2030 against 138 in May.

Forward thesis

Carry a range for the 2030s carbon price, not one scarcity curve. The tripwire is the Council or Parliament setting the 2031 to 2035 reduction factor at or below 3.7% before 31 March 2027.

Shift 6 of 6 Split

Nature risk has left the sustainability report for the risk register

Prior cycle held

The September synthesis held that "nature risk moves from the sustainability report to the risk register", and advised keeping nature exposure out of the reporting budget.

This cycle reads

True in Europe and false where ISSB standards apply. The EU cut mandatory reporting datapoints by over 60%, but the ISSB states that material nature disclosure "is not optional; IFRS S1 already requires that", and TNFD-aligned reporters doubled to over 1,000 organisations.

Forward thesis

Run a nature materiality screen in ISSB jurisdictions even where no nature standard is mandatory. The tripwire is an ISSB jurisdiction referencing the final Practice Statement in its own rules.

Primary decision ownerChief procurement officer, with commodity riskDecision windowNext quarter2027 cover levels and second origins by 31 January 2027
Why this now matters

The market answers a price rise with more planting only after a season, so the cover bought against today's stocks is cheaper than the cover bought against next spring's harvest estimates.

Recommended actions
  • Immediate: re-size cover for each El Niño-exposed commodity to exporters' stocks and the FAO cereal index rather than world totals.
  • Immediate: qualify a second origin for each, outside the belt El Niño dries, such as Brazil's record crop.
  • Next quarter: add drought area, reservoir storage and sowing reports to the triggers for further purchases.
  • Follow-on: write export-restriction clauses into supply contracts with South and Southeast Asian origins.
Review conditions
  • EscalateIf a grain-exporting government restricts wheat, maize or rice exports for a month or more before 31 March 2027, extend forward cover to two quarters and move purchases to the second origins.
  • Scale backIf USDA shows major exporters' 2026/27 corn ending stocks back above 27.14 million tonnes, keep the second origins but hold cover at today's level.
  • Stand downIf NOAA lowers the chance of a very strong event below 50% and the FAO cereal index is no more than 5% above a year earlier in January 2027, return to normal cover.
