Why This Matters Now

Four dates governed this organisation's plan in July. Three of them have since moved. The tax on actual investment returns, due to start in 2028, is now waiting on a coalition decision deferred to next spring. The European deadline that was to force the clean-up of automated selection slipped by sixteen months. A promise to simplify five hundred rules a year had already been missed before it was written into a bill.

The fourth date held. On 1 July the European Union began charging duty on small parcels. Within two months the parcels stopped arriving as parcels: customs declarations fell by nearly half, and the item lines inside them by seven-tenths, as goods were consolidated into European warehouses and sent on from there. The charge changed the route rather than the receipts.

The one thing to take into the next meeting: when a deadline moves, the work behind it does not disappear. It becomes undated. Undated work cannot be staffed, sequenced or stopped, and it is the most expensive kind an organisation can hold.

Executive Synthesis

Which of the dates in your 2027 plan are fixed, and which are somebody else's opening position?

Three of the four deadlines this administration was planning against moved within ten weeks, and the only one that held redirected the flow it was designed to tax.

The Belastingdienst spent this quarter losing its calendar. On 30 June the Eerste Kamer held over its vote on the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 until the cabinet produces the novelle it had promised (Eerste Kamer, Jun 2026). On 11 September the decision moved again, to the Voorjaarsnota, because the three coalition parties could not agree what to change or how to pay for it: in Dutch, “D66, VVD en CDA zijn er na verder overleg niet in geslaagd overeenstemming te bereiken over aanpassingen van de belasting op sparen en beleggen en de miljarden dekking daarvoor.”, which reads in English as “D66, VVD and CDA have not succeeded, after further discussion, in reaching agreement on adjustments to the tax on saving and investing and on the billions in funding for it” (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026). The Belastingplan 2027 package tabled on Prinsjesdag contains no box 3 measure at all (Rijksoverheid, Sep 2026), while the government's own explainer page, last revised on 29 July, still tells readers the repair will be in that package and that the new system begins on 1 January 2028 (Rijksoverheid, Jul 2026). Three official positions, none reconciled, and a programme that can now be neither cancelled nor committed.

The same quarter moved a second date. The obligation that framed our July edition, EU AI Act high-risk duties falling on public-authority enforcement systems from 2 August 2026, was postponed by sixteen months when (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Jul 2026) entered into force on 27 July, shifting Annex III systems to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028 (the European Commission, Jul 2026). And a third: the cabinet's pledge of an annual Vereenvoudigingswet stripping at least five hundred rules a year (the Troonrede, Sep 2026) arrived three months after a ministerial letter reported that the first such target had been missed, at rather more than four hundred (Financieel Management, Jun 2026).

One date held, and it is the instructive one. The abolition of the EUR 150 customs de-minimis and the interim EUR 3 duty took effect on 1 July exactly as the Council legislated in February (the Council of the EU, Feb 2026). Two months later the Douane reported in Dutch, “In juli en augustus zag Douane Nederland het gemiddelde aantal aangiften e-commerce afnemen met 46% ten opzichte van het gemiddelde aantal van de eerste 6 maanden van 2026. Tegelijkertijd daalde het aantal aangifteregels e-commerce met 70%.”, which reads in English as “In July and August Douane Nederland saw the average number of e-commerce declarations fall by 46% against the average for the first 6 months of 2026. At the same time the number of e-commerce declaration lines fell by 70%” (Douane Nederland, Sep 2026). Regular declarations rose 20% and their lines 30% over the same period. Nothing was smuggled and nothing stopped: the goods were consolidated into warehouses inside the European Union and despatched from there, a pattern French customs and Belgian trade reporting both show (Euronews, Aug 2026) (Flows, Aug 2026). The measure that arrived on time did not raise the revenue it was aimed at. It moved the traffic.

A postponed programme does not release the people held against it. It converts them into a standing reserve for work that nobody has yet authorised.

What did not move is the work. To 19 August the Belastingdienst had received 863,600 objection forms under the box 3 tegenbewijsregeling, up some 60,000 in the ten weeks since 8 June, with 537,800 substantively processed and EUR 969 million repaid against the EUR 16.6 billion successive cabinets had reserved (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026) (Taxence, Jul 2026). Roughly 700 full-time staff are engaged on it and the operation is expected to run until at least 2030. In the Senate debate the uitvoeringstoets for the new system was put on the record as requiring in Dutch, “De uitvoeringstoets laat zien dat het stelsel een enorme belasting legt op de Belastingdienst. We hebben het niet over een paar extra medewerkers. Nee, we hebben het over bijna 900 extra fte structureel, boven op de bestaande wervingsopgave.”, which reads in English as “The implementation assessment shows that the system places an enormous burden on the Belastingdienst. We are not talking about a few extra staff. No, we are talking about nearly 900 extra FTE structurally, on top of the existing recruitment task” (Eerste Kamer, Handelingen, Jun 2026). Postponement subtracts nothing from either number. It only removes the date against which they could be planned.

Underneath sits the constraint the Algemene Rekenkamer named in May: in Dutch, “Deze systemen zijn nodig om € 233,6 miljard aan belastingen en premies te heffen. Het risico ontstaat dat de Belastingdienst deze ontvangsten niet kan heffen wanneer deze systemen op den duur het heffingsproces niet meer kunnen ondersteunen.”, which reads in English as “These systems are needed to levy EUR 233.6 billion in taxes and contributions. The risk arises that the Belastingdienst cannot levy these receipts when in time these systems can no longer support the levying process” (the Algemene Rekenkamer, May 2026). That is the machine on which a deferred reform, a missed simplification target, an algorithm remediation programme whose external deadline has just slipped, and a 2030 e-invoicing mandate that has not slipped, all have to be sequenced. The Belastingdienst's own Stand van de uitvoering is unusually direct about where the lever sits: in Dutch, “De Belastingdienst kan dit niet alleen oplossen. Veel knelpunten hebben hun oorsprong in wet- en regelgeving en vereisen daarom politieke keuzes.”, which reads in English as “The Belastingdienst cannot solve this alone. Many bottlenecks have their origin in legislation and regulation and therefore require political choices” (the Belastingdienst, Jun 2026).

Where this analysis could be wrong

Delay is the normal condition of Dutch tax legislation, and the organisation has absorbed it before. Dates have slipped on box 3 since the 2021 Hoge Raad ruling without collection failing, and an argument that this particular slip is different has to carry that history. The honest form of our claim is narrower than the headline: the cost is not the delay, it is the combination of an undated build with a legacy tail that is independently committed to 2030.

Two of our load-bearing numbers reach us at one remove. The figure of nearly 900 extra FTE comes from a senator quoting the uitvoeringstoets on the floor of the Eerste Kamer, not from the document, whose PDF we could not open. The EUR 2.4 billion to EUR 2.5 billion of annual revenue said to be at stake rests on leaked Prinsjesdag papers and unnamed sources in two outlets (Taxence, Sep 2026) (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026), not on a published raming. We also record a trap we came close to walking into: EUR 2.4 billion a year is separately the reported cost of the tegenbewijsregeling itself (Eerste Kamer, Handelingen, Jun 2026). The two figures coincide by accident and mean different things.

The customs finding is two months old, and the comparisons do not converge. July and August are measured against a six-month average that includes no summer. The Netherlands reports a 46% fall in declarations; NOS rendered the same figure as a fall in parcels (NOS, Sep 2026); Belgium reports 35% of low-value Chinese imports (Flows, Aug 2026) and French customs 30% to 40% of parcels (Euronews, Aug 2026). These are different quantities over different periods and we have not averaged them. France is also not a clean control: it levied its own EUR 2 per-article parcel tax from 1 March 2026, repealed when the EU duty began (French customs, Jul 2026).

The decisions that cannot be deferred this cycle

  1. Put a date on the undated. Decide now what the box 3 build does between today and the Voorjaarsnota, rather than holding it ready.
  2. Stop treating the AI Act as the clock. The court exposure and the AP's conditions run on national law, which did not move by sixteen months.
  3. Read the parcel numbers as a demand signal, not a revenue one. The line count, not the declaration count, is what the November handling fee will be charged against.
  4. Book the 2030 e-invoicing decision into 2027. Parliamentary handling has to be finished by 1 July 2028, which makes this a present-tense commitment.

Each is developed below, with a decision posture, in the four Strategic Implications.

Themes

The cycle's signals are organised into six themes, ranked by impact on near-term decisions. Immediate: changes the near-term plan, the structure or the proposition. Near-Term: changes position over the next twelve months. Longer-Range: a multi-year factor to track and revisit each cycle.

1. Postponing Box 3 does not give the capacity back

Immediate

The reform that has organised this administration's planning for four years now has no date. The Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 passed the Tweede Kamer on 12 February. On 30 June the Eerste Kamer, rather than voting it down, held the vote over until the cabinet delivers the novelle it promised (Eerste Kamer, Jun 2026). On 11 September the cabinet declined to deliver it and moved the decision to the Voorjaarsnota (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026). The bill is therefore alive, unpassed and unscheduled at once, which is the single most expensive state a large programme can occupy. It cannot be stopped, so its people cannot be released. It cannot be committed, so its work cannot begin.

Three of the four dates that framed the July cycle have moved. The one that held is the one nobody described as reform.

  • The Belastingplan 2027 package tabled on Prinsjesdag contains no box 3 measure (Rijksoverheid, Sep 2026), although the government's own explainer page, last revised 29 July, still states that any repair will be in that package and that the system starts on 1 January 2028 (Rijksoverheid, Jul 2026).
  • The legacy tail is independent of the new system and is growing. To 19 August the Belastingdienst had received 863,600 objection forms under the tegenbewijsregeling, against 804,411 on 8 June, with 537,800 substantively processed (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026). Around 700 full-time staff are engaged and the operation is expected to run until at least 2030 (Taxence, Jul 2026).
  • Against EUR 16.6 billion reserved by successive cabinets for box 3 restoration, EUR 969 million has been repaid (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026). The underspend is not a saving. It is a measure of how much processing has not yet happened.
  • In the Senate debate the uitvoeringstoets for the new system was quoted as requiring nearly 900 additional full-time staff on top of the existing recruitment task, with the tegenbewijsregeling separately said to absorb close to 2,000 more (Eerste Kamer, Handelingen, Jun 2026).
  • The postponement does not stay inside box 3. From 2028 the zorgtoeslag wealth limits were to be set equal to the heffingsvrij vermogen in box 3, and the deduction for specifieke zorgkosten abolished (PwC, Jan 2026). Dienst Toeslagen's 2028 build now depends on a parameter of a bill nobody can schedule.

Early indicators

  • Weak signal The cabinet is researching a vermogenswinstbelasting in parallel. A realisation-based system is not a softened version of the current bill but a different machine, with a different data supply and a different uitvoeringstoets (Rijksoverheid, Jul 2026). If that is where the Voorjaarsnota lands, the 900 FTE figure is a floor rather than an estimate.

Counter-argument

Dates have slipped on box 3 since 2021 without collection failing, and an organisation that has absorbed five years of slippage will absorb a sixth. That is fair, and it is why the claim here is narrower than the headline: the cost is not the delay but the collision of an undated build with a legacy operation already committed to 2030. The thesis would weaken if the Voorjaarsnota produced a dated decision that the Eerste Kamer accepted without a further novelle.

Decision link: SI 1. The question is not when box 3 starts, but what the build does between now and the Voorjaarsnota.

2. The new parcel charge moved the parcels, not the revenue

Immediate

On 1 July the European Union abolished the EUR 150 customs de-minimis and began levying an interim flat duty of EUR 3, charged per item category rather than per parcel, running to 1 July 2028 (the Council of the EU, Feb 2026) (the European Commission, Jun 2026). It is the only deadline in this cycle that arrived on the day it was legislated for, and it produced the cycle's most useful finding: a compliance instrument does not simply tax a flow, it reshapes it. Two months of Dutch data show the flow reshaping faster than any forecast contemplated.

The parcels did not stop. They were consolidated into European warehouses and entered as bulk, which is why the two columns move in opposite directions.

  • E-commerce declarations fell 46% in July and August against the average of the first six months of the year, and the item lines inside them fell 70%, while regular declarations rose 20% and their lines 30% (Douane Nederland, Sep 2026).
  • The mechanism is consolidation, not evasion. Non-EU retailers are importing in bulk into European warehouses and despatching to consumers from inside the customs union, a pattern the Douane says it also observes in France and Belgium (Douane Nederland, Sep 2026).
  • The platform response is uneven. Volume sales between June and July fell 50% at Temu and 37% at AliExpress but only 15% at Shein, which opened a Polish warehouse in late 2025 (Euronews, Aug 2026). The charge is sorting platforms by whether they already hold EU capacity.
  • The revenue is small and the baseline is unknown. The duty produced around EUR 35 million in the Netherlands in July (NOS, Sep 2026). We could not locate any published forecast against which to judge that, and we do not present it as an overshoot or a shortfall.
  • The next instrument is priced against the quantity that collapsed. From 1 November a European handling fee of EUR 2 applies to every declaration line (Thuiswinkel.org, Jun 2026), and declaration lines are down 70%.

Early indicators

  • Weak signal The interim duty has a scheduled successor, and it is a data system. The EUR 3 rate is explicitly a placeholder until the EU customs data hub is operational, when normal tariffs resume (the Council of the EU, Feb 2026). That converts a rate question into an integration question on the same 2028 horizon as everything else in this cycle.

Counter-argument

Two months is a short series, the comparison base excludes any previous summer, and the national figures do not converge: the Netherlands reports 46% on declarations, Belgium 35% on low-value Chinese imports (Flows, Aug 2026) and French customs 30% to 40% on parcels (Euronews, Aug 2026). France is also not a clean control, having levied its own EUR 2 per-article tax from 1 March 2026 until the EU duty replaced it (French customs, Jul 2026). The thesis would weaken if declaration volumes recovered through the autumn peak without a matching rise in bulk import.

Decision link: SI 3. Forecast against the line count, not the declaration count.

3. The AI Act deadline moved to December 2027. The algorithm problem stayed.

Immediate

The July cycle built its second theme on 2 August 2026, the date EU AI Act high-risk obligations were to bind public-authority enforcement systems. That date is gone. (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Jul 2026), adopted on 8 July and in force from 27 July, moved Annex III systems to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028 (the European Commission, Jul 2026). Sixteen months of slack has appeared in a remediation programme that was being driven by the deadline. Nothing else that constrains the programme moved at all.

  • The transparency duties took effect on schedule: in Dutch, “Artikel 50 van de AI-verordening is vanaf 2 augustus 2026 van toepassing.”, which reads in English as “Article 50 of the AI Regulation applies from 2 August 2026” (PrimeLaw, Aug 2026), with a narrow carve-out postponing the marking requirement for generative systems already on the market to 2 December 2026 (IT en Recht, Jul 2026).
  • The national supervisor did not relax. On 2 September the AP permitted the Belastingdienst to process special-category data for bias and fairness testing, but only so far as strictly necessary, and in Dutch, “De AP benadrukt dat dit een tijdelijke oplossing is, benodigd voor de uitvoering van het Plan van Aanpak voor de toetsing van de geautomatiseerde selectie-instrumenten. Het is de bedoeling dat de Belastingdienst in de tussentijd op zoek gaat naar een structurele oplossing voor verwerking van (bijzondere) persoonsgegevens bij bias- en fairness-toetsing.”, which reads in English as “The AP stresses that this is a temporary solution, needed for the execution of the Action Plan for the assessment of the automated selection instruments. The intention is that in the meantime the Belastingdienst looks for a structural solution for processing (special categories of) personal data in bias and fairness testing” (Computable, Sep 2026).
  • The remediation programme has paperwork but no published deadline of its own. The Plan van aanpak Geautomatiseerde Selectie-instrumenten and the AP's assessment of it went to the Tweede Kamer in March (the Ministerie van Financiën, Mar 2026), and the AP's September decision is framed entirely around executing that plan (Computable, Sep 2026). No completion date attaches to either.
  • The exposure runs through national administrative law, which did not move. After April and May rulings that the IND had insufficiently explained its CaseMatcher algorithm, Follow the Money argues the Belastingdienst faces the same risk across millions of decisions (Follow the Money, Jun 2026).
  • The register and the regulator do not describe the same estate. The national register lists 69 published algorithms, every one marked as in use (the national algorithm register, accessed Sep 2026), while the Belastingdienst is reported to run at least 68, of which the AP found more than 50 unlawful and discriminatory (Follow the Money, Jun 2026). Registered algorithms and automated selection instruments are different populations and we have not reconciled them.
  • Dutch supervision itself is late. The draft Uitvoeringswet designates ten market surveillance authorities and the cabinet concedes it will enter into force later than the Regulation requires (Binnenlands Bestuur, Apr 2026).

Early indicators

  • Weak signal A temporary permission with no expiry date attached. The AP's decision is explicitly temporary and conditional on pursuing a permanent alternative, possibly through experimenteerwetgeving (Computable, Sep 2026). No date is attached to either the permission or the replacement. That is a second undated obligation, inside a programme that just lost its only dated one.

Counter-argument

Sixteen months of genuine slack in a portfolio the Rekenkamer describes as overloaded is a real gain, and treating a postponement as a risk can read as refusing good news. The distinction that matters is whether the slack is reallocated by decision or absorbed by default. The thesis would weaken if the Belastingdienst published a remediation plan with dates of its own, independent of the AI Act calendar.

Decision link: SI 2. Replace the external date with an internal one, or the programme has no clock.

4. The government has promised to simplify 500 rules a year

Near-Term

The Troonrede of 15 September committed the cabinet to an annual Vereenvoudigingswet with a numeric target: at least five hundred rules disappearing or becoming simpler each year (the Troonrede, Sep 2026). A counted annual target is a different kind of obligation from a policy intention. It is auditable, it recurs, and it competes for the same legislative and execution slots as everything already queued. Three facts about it were on the public record before it was announced.

  • The target had already been missed. In a letter to the Tweede Kamer in June the minister reported that the cabinet had scrapped or simplified just over four hundred rules against the promised five hundred (Financieel Management, Jun 2026).
  • The instrument excludes the category the Belastingdienst's problems fall into. The Kamerbrief setting out the mechanism states in Dutch, “De wet is niet bedoeld voor grote stelselwijzigingen of politiek gevoelige onderwerpen.”, which reads in English as “The Act is not intended for major system changes or politically sensitive subjects” (Rijksoverheid, Sep 2026).
  • The first edition contains no fiscal content. It covers the Omgevingswet, the Burgerlijk Wetboek, the Energiewet, the Mijnbouwwet, offshore wind and student finance (Rijksoverheid, Sep 2026), drawn from four ministries, of which Financiën is not one (TaxLive, Sep 2026).
  • The state's own legislative adviser recorded the contradiction on the same day. On the Belastingplan 2027 the Raad van State wrote in Dutch, “In het pakket Belastingplan 2027 worden een aantal maatregelen voorgesteld met het oog op vereenvoudiging van het belastingstelsel, waaronder aanpassing of afschaffing van enkele negatief geëvalueerde fiscale regelingen. Ook worden meerdere maatregelen voorgesteld die complexiteitsverhogend zijn.”, which reads in English as “A number of measures are proposed in the Belastingplan 2027 package with a view to simplifying the tax system, including the adjustment or abolition of several negatively evaluated fiscal arrangements. Multiple measures are also proposed that increase complexity” (the Raad van State, Sep 2026), and noted Belastingdienst reservations about enforceability and fraud resistance.
  • The same coalition agreement that promises the 500 also imposes an annual efficiency target on delivery organisations, of which only half may be reinvested in improvement (the coalition agreement, Feb 2026).

Counter-argument

A shortfall of four hundred against five hundred in the first year of a new instrument is not a failure, and compressing consultation from six weeks to four is a genuine throughput gain. The objection is one of arithmetic rather than intent: a target counted in rules rewards removing many small ones, while the Belastingdienst's own bottlenecks are few and large. The thesis would weaken if the first Vereenvoudigingswet carried a fiscal chapter addressing a named knelpunt from the Stand van de uitvoering.

Decision link: SI 4. Get a Financiën chapter into the second edition, or the quota is somebody else's programme.

5. Tax advisers and neighbouring administrations automated first

Near-Term

While Dutch automated selection sits under a conditional and temporary permission, the people on the other side of the assessment and the administrations across the border have been moving. The asymmetry is not primarily about capability. It is about who currently has permission to act, and the answer is: everybody else.

  • A survey of 799 practitioners fielded in April found roughly 80% of junior tax advisers using AI daily, around 70% of mediors and 53% of seniors, with 60% among in-house corporate tax specialists (Accountant.nl, Sep 2026).
  • Governance has not kept up on that side either. Only about 25% of respondents report written AI guidelines in their firm (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026). The Belastingdienst is therefore meeting submissions prepared with tools their own preparers do not formally govern.
  • North Rhine-Westphalia put self-developed AI risk selection into every one of its tax offices in spring 2026 and the component has been available across Germany since February (the North Rhine-Westphalia tax administration, Jun 2026). The ministry's stated expectation: in German, “Landesweit rechnen wir im neuen Jahr mit rund 800.000 weniger Prüfhinweisen, weil die Künstliche Intelligenz gleichzeitig auch Fälle mit geringem Risiko anhand gut nachvollziehbarer Muster treffsicher identifiziert.”, which reads in English as “Across the state we expect around 800,000 fewer audit flags in the new year, because the artificial intelligence simultaneously identifies low-risk cases accurately on the basis of clearly traceable patterns” (dpa-AFX, Apr 2026).
  • It won an award for it. The module took the Public Leadership Award in June 2026 (the North Rhine-Westphalia tax administration, Jun 2026). The same class of automated selection that is restricted here is being held up as a model of responsible public sector AI next door.
  • Berlin is following, with robotic process automation in the Uebernachtungsteuer unit since January and two AI research tools released to all tax office staff in May (the Berlin Senate, Jul 2026).
  • The direction is European, and so is the caveat. The Commission reports that 22% of its Technical Support Instrument projects target tax compliance and a further 21% the digitalisation of revenue administration, while stating that enforcement capacity cannot substitute for tax morale and public trust (the European Commission, Jul 2026).

Early indicators

  • Weak signal Germany separately shipped a one-click return, which is a different programme. From 1 July 2026 around 11.5 million German citizens can file a pre-filled return with a preview of the assessment in a single action (the state of North Rhine-Westphalia, Jun 2026). It is easy to read this and the risk-selection rollout as one story. They are two, and only one of them depends on selection algorithms.

Counter-argument

The Netherlands has specific reasons for caution that Germany does not, and the toeslagenaffaire is the reason. An administration that moved fast on automated selection and was found to have discriminated should be slower the second time. The honest question is not whether to be slower but whether the current pace is a chosen pace. The thesis would weaken if the AP's conditions were shown to be compatible with deployment at the scale NRW has reached.

Decision link: SI 2. The comparator is not last year's Belastingdienst, it is this year's Finanzamt.

6. E-invoicing in 2030 is a decision that has to be made now

Longer-Range

One date in this cycle comes from EU law rather than from a coalition, and it has not moved. (Council Directive (EU) 2025/516, Mar 2025) fixes the ViDA digital reporting regime from 1 July 2030. On 11 September the cabinet set the Dutch shape of it: mandatory B2B e-invoicing from 1 July 2030 and digital reporting to the Belastingdienst from 1 July 2031 (Rijksoverheid, Sep 2026). The date that matters for this organisation is none of those three.

  • Parliamentary handling has to be complete before 1 July 2028, with internet consultation starting this autumn and the bill expected before the 2027 summer recess (Taxence, Sep 2026). That is the real deadline, and it falls inside the current planning period.
  • Firms under the kleineondernemersregeling, with turnover up to EUR 20,000, are exempt from both obligations (Rijksoverheid, Sep 2026), which means the reporting population has to be identified and maintained rather than assumed.
  • Near-real-time reporting is a different machine from periodic returns. The EY research commissioned by the ministry recommends a phased introduction with substantial time between publication of the legislation and go-live (EY, Mar 2026), and cites per-invoice cost savings of 55% to 70% against paper handling (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026).
  • France is the live comparator and it is already running. From 1 September 2026 every French business in scope must be able to receive electronic invoices: in French, “À compter du 1er septembre 2026, toutes les entreprises concernées par la réforme doivent avoir la capacité de recevoir des factures électroniques.”, which reads in English as “From 1 September 2026, all businesses covered by the reform must have the capacity to receive electronic invoices” (the French Direction generale des Finances publiques, Jul 2026), with small firms issuing from 1 September 2027.
  • The receiving side is a systems project rather than a filing change, and Dutch business representatives are already describing it that way (evofenedex, Sep 2026).

Counter-argument

2030 is four years out, the Belastingdienst has more urgent work, and building early against a standard still being specified wastes effort. That is right about the build and wrong about the decision: the architecture choice has to survive legislative scrutiny by mid-2028, which is two planning cycles away, not four. The thesis would weaken if the ViDA dates themselves slipped, which would put every deadline in this cycle in the same category.

Decision link: SI 4. Treat 1 July 2028 as the date, not 1 July 2030.

Strategic Implications

Four implications, each carrying a decision posture. This cycle produced no monitor-only implication, which is itself a finding: every one of the four has an action that is cheaper taken now than taken after the Voorjaarsnota.

SI 1: Decide what the box 3 build does between now and the Voorjaarsnota

The programme is alive, unpassed and unscheduled (Eerste Kamer, Jun 2026) (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026). Held ready, it consumes architects, testers and legislative capacity at close to full cost while producing nothing; stood down, it cannot restart inside the window a spring decision would leave. The choice between those two is currently being made by default, quarter by quarter, by nobody in particular. The dependent builds make the default worse: Dienst Toeslagen's 2028 zorgtoeslag change is pegged to a box 3 parameter that no longer has a settled value (PwC, Jan 2026).

Action: Name an accountable owner for the box 3 programme's holding state and publish, internally, what it will and will not do before the Voorjaarsnota. Attach a standing monthly cost to holding it ready, so that the next deferral is priced rather than absorbed.

Decide Draws on Theme 1

SI 2: Give algorithm remediation a date of its own

The AI Act was never the binding constraint; it was the visible one. Its Annex III obligations moved to 2 December 2027 (the European Commission, Jul 2026), while the AP's conditions, the national administrative-law exposure and the transparency duties that took effect on 2 August 2026 all stayed where they were (Computable, Sep 2026) (Follow the Money, Jun 2026) (PrimeLaw, Aug 2026). Sixteen months of slack in an overloaded portfolio will be absorbed by something. The only question is whether that happens by decision. Meanwhile North Rhine-Westphalia has taken the same class of automated selection to every one of its offices (the North Rhine-Westphalia tax administration, Jun 2026).

Action: Publish a remediation timetable with internal dates that would still stand if the AI Act had never been amended, and state explicitly what the AP's temporary permission is temporary until.

Decide Draws on Themes 3 and 5

SI 3: Rebase customs forecasting on declaration lines

Two months of data show the EUR 3 duty redirecting trade rather than taxing it in place: e-commerce declarations down 46% and their lines down 70%, with regular declarations up 20% (Douane Nederland, Sep 2026). The European handling fee arriving on 1 November is charged per declaration line (Thuiswinkel.org, Jun 2026), which is the quantity that has fallen furthest. Any receipts forecast built on parcel counts, or on the declaration count, is forecasting the wrong variable. The same caution applies to the capacity plan: the work moved from many small declarations to fewer large ones, which is a different staffing shape, not a smaller one.

Action: Rebuild the November handling-fee forecast on declaration lines and publish the assumed recovery through the autumn peak, so the assumption can be falsified in December rather than defended in March.

Prepare Draws on Theme 2

SI 4: Book the e-invoicing architecture decision into 2027, and get Financiën into the Vereenvoudigingswet

Parliamentary handling of the e-invoicing mandate must finish before 1 July 2028, with consultation opening this autumn (Taxence, Sep 2026). That is the operative date, not 2030, and France is already running the receive-side of the same reform (the French Direction generale des Finances publiques, Jul 2026). In parallel, the annual simplification quota is currently somebody else's programme: the first edition carries no fiscal content and excludes major system changes (Rijksoverheid, Sep 2026), while the Raad van State records that the Belastingplan 2027 adds complexity as well as removing it (the Raad van State, Sep 2026).

Action: Put the reporting architecture choice on a 2027 decision calendar with the legislative deadline attached, and submit at least one named knelpunt from the Stand van de uitvoering (the Belastingdienst, Jun 2026) into the second edition of the Vereenvoudigingswet.

The organisation stops waiting

No decision arrives at the Voorjaarsnota, but the Belastingdienst stands the box 3 build down anyway and moves its people onto legacy retirement and the 2030 reporting architecture. Box 3 will then arrive late and be built fast, which is how the toeslagen systems were built. The gain is real and the risk is specific.

Early indicators: An internal remediation timetable published with dates independent of the AI Act calendar; a named owner for the box 3 holding state; e-invoicing consultation responses citing architecture rather than scope.

The backlog thins and the build starts

The Voorjaarsnota settles box 3 with cover, the Eerste Kamer accepts the novelle, and the tegenbewijsregeling inflow finally falls below its processing rate. Capacity moves in sequence rather than by triage. This is the only quadrant in which the 2030 e-invoicing mandate gets a normal runway.

Early indicators: A dated Kamerbrief on box 3 before April; objection inflow below 20,000 a month; the Rekenkamer closing rather than adding imperfections in its 2027 audit.

Standing reserve

The most likely continuation of the present state. Capacity stays earmarked for a build nobody authorises, the AI Act slack is absorbed without being reallocated, the simplification quota consumes legislative slots that Financiën has no claim on, and the e-invoicing runway shortens from four years to two while nobody records that it has.

Early indicators: A second deferral at the Voorjaarsnota; no fiscal chapter in the second Vereenvoudigingswet; the AP's temporary permission passing its first anniversary with no replacement named.

A decision that costs more than the delay did

The coalition settles on a realisation-based vermogenswinstbelasting rather than the current bill (Rijksoverheid, Jul 2026). That is not a softened version of the same system but a different one, with a different data supply, and the uitvoeringstoets has to be run again. The date arrives and the capacity does not, because the thing now being built is larger than the thing that was costed.

Early indicators: Voorjaarsnota language naming gerealiseerde vermogenswinst; a fresh uitvoeringstoets commissioned; the standing FTE requirement revised above the 900 quoted in the Senate.

What We Are Not Planning For

Political discontinuity

We are not planning for the coalition falling before the Voorjaarsnota

Three parties failed to agree on box 3 and chose deferral rather than rupture (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026). Deferral is what a coalition does when it intends to continue. We treat the spring decision point as real and the government as durable to it, which means we are not modelling a caretaker period in which no fiscal decision of any kind can be taken.

↺ Reinstated if a confidence motion is tabled, or a second deferral is announced without a new date attached.

Regulatory reversal

We are not planning for the AI Act postponement being reversed

(Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Jul 2026) is in force and the Commission has confirmed the new dates (the European Commission, Jul 2026). Pressure to restore the original timetable exists but a second amendment inside eighteen months would be unusual. We plan on 2 December 2027 standing, while noting that nothing in this brief depends on it: the constraints that bind are national and did not move.

↺ Reinstated if the Parliament opens a procedure to reverse the Annex III deferral, or a member state legislates its original date domestically.

Trade retaliation

We are not planning for the parcel duty being withdrawn under trade pressure

The EUR 3 rate is explicitly interim and expires on 1 July 2028 when normal tariffs resume under the customs data hub (the Council of the EU, Feb 2026). An early withdrawal under pressure from affected exporters would undo the volume shift, but the measure has just cleared unanimous Council adoption and member states are collecting receipts under it. We plan on it running its term.

↺ Reinstated if a WTO challenge is accepted, or the Commission proposes an early replacement of the flat rate.

Claim-fidelity self-disclosure

This briefing rests on 51 sources, every one of which was opened and read rather than taken from a search result. 37 are in Dutch, 7 in English, 4 in German and 3 in French. Twelve spans are quoted word for word; the rest are summarised. Where we summarise, we have tried not to make a source say more than it says. This box records the places where that discipline was hardest.

Claims that reach us at one remove

The figure of nearly 900 extra full-time staff for the new box 3 system comes from a senator quoting the uitvoeringstoets during the Eerste Kamer debate of 30 June (Eerste Kamer, Handelingen, Jun 2026), not from the assessment itself, whose PDF is not machine-retrievable. The same applies to the objection-form volumes and the 700 staff on restoration: those come from two trade outlets reporting Kamerbrieven (Taxence, Jul 2026) (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026) rather than from the letters. The Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens' own website could not be reached at all during preparation, so its 2 September decision is carried by press reporting (Computable, Sep 2026). We have not published an AP link we did not open.

A figure we did not publish

The EUR 2.4 billion to EUR 2.5 billion of annual revenue attributed to the box 3 postponement rests on leaked Prinsjesdag papers and unnamed sources in two outlets (Taxence, Sep 2026) (Accountancy Vanmorgen, Sep 2026). We report it as a range and attribute it, and we do not present it as a Ministry of Finance estimate. Separately, a reported figure of around EUR 20 billion for box 3 scenarios circulated on 16 September; we could not open either outlet carrying it and it appears nowhere in this brief.

Two numbers that collide

EUR 2.4 billion a year is also the reported annual cost of the tegenbewijsregeling (Eerste Kamer, Handelingen, Jun 2026). It is a different quantity from the revenue forgone by not starting the new system, and the coincidence is accidental. We came close to merging them and record that here because a reader recombining our figures could make the same mistake.

Counts we have deliberately left unreconciled

The number of Belastingdienst algorithms is given as 69 in the national register, every one marked as in use (the national algorithm register, accessed Sep 2026), and as at least 68 with more than 50 found unlawful and discriminatory by the data protection authority (Follow the Money, Jun 2026). Registered algorithms and automated selection instruments are different populations and we have not averaged them. Likewise the parcel-volume falls: 46% of declarations in the Netherlands (Douane Nederland, Sep 2026), 35% of low-value Chinese imports in Belgium (Flows, Aug 2026), 30% to 40% of parcels EU-wide per French customs (Euronews, Aug 2026). NOS rendered the Dutch 46% as a fall in parcels rather than declarations (NOS, Sep 2026); we have used the Douane's own wording.

What we corrected before publication

Our preliminary reading treated the box 3 bill as shelved. The Eerste Kamer's own record says the vote was held over pending a novelle (Eerste Kamer, Jun 2026), which is a materially different status, and the brief was rewritten around it. We also initially treated North Rhine-Westphalia's AI risk selection and Germany's one-click return as one programme. They are two, launched separately (the North Rhine-Westphalia tax administration, Jun 2026) (the state of North Rhine-Westphalia, Jun 2026), and conflating them would have overstated the comparison.
