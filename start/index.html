How it works

A typical programme tracks four areas of change you care about (technology shifts, regulatory pressure, customer behaviour, competitive moves, supply-chain risk, anything). The cadence below repeats every quarter; the depth deepens as memory accumulates across cycles.

Weekly

Signal Scan

One weak signal beneath the headlines. The non-obvious development we think you should pay attention to this week.

~1,300 words · 5-min read

Monthly

Change Tracker

A synthesis of what shifted across your tracked areas this month, drawing on the month's scans plus any external developments worth flagging.

~1,200 words · 5-min read

Quarterly

Decision Intelligence Briefing

A board-grade briefing covering all four of your tracked areas as themes: signal clusters, leadership snapshots, scenario matrix, strategic implications.

~6,000 words · 20-min read

End of quarter

Action Triggers

One per topic, after the quarterly briefing ships. The operational handoff: what your team should watch, prepare for, or decide on next quarter.

~1,800 words · 7-min read

What happens next

Four touchpoints, roughly two weeks from your first form submission to the first Signal Scan landing in your inbox.

  1. You submit the form below. We read it the same day and reply within one business day to confirm fit and propose a Discovery call time.
  2. Discovery call (60 minutes). A short, structured conversation where we help you define the four areas your programme should track. You think out loud, we listen and group.
  3. We send back a written programme proposal. Two pages: your four areas translated into defined topics, a 12-month calendar showing every scan, tracker, briefing and Action Triggers date, plus distribution and branding confirmations. You sign off or ask for edits.
  4. Your programme begins the following Monday. First Signal Scan arrives that week. First Change Tracker at the end of the month. First Decision Intelligence briefing at the natural quarter end.
