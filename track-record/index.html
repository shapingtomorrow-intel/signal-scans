Track record

Every call our briefings make, graded later against what happened. Misses included.

Each Decision Intelligence edition makes calls about where a topic is heading and sets milestones to watch, and each Signal Scan states how likely its signal is to bite within a dated window. A later edition grades each one against what has happened since. We copy each grade here and do not change it afterwards. Calls that went against us stay on the record, and a call that is not yet settled is not counted as a hit.

What the grades mean

  • Happened. Events went the way the call said, or the milestone arrived as expected.
  • Partly. Part of the call held; part was recast or did not happen.
  • On track. Moving the way the call said, but the decisive event has not happened yet.
  • Not yet settled. The evidence so far does not settle it either way.
  • Didn't happen. Events went against the call, or the milestone did not arrive as expected.
  • Due for a grade. Its window has opened, or the next edition on its topic is due, so the next Change Tracker or briefing grades it against what has happened.
  • Not yet due. On the record from the day it was made, so it cannot be quietly dropped, but its window has not opened yet. It is graded once it does.
